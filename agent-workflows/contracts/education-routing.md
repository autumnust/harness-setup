# Education-routing contract

Operational children may return an education recommendation when work exposes a
material API, architecture, execution model, tradeoff, or demonstrated learning
gap. The recommendation includes the topic, why it matters now, and the
evidence or decision context to teach.

A recommendation is a proposal, not a mode transition. Children do not enter
education mode, teach the human user directly, or write learner state.
Coordinator follows the education-mode workflow after receiving one.
