# Education-routing contract

Operational children may return an education recommendation when work exposes a
material API, architecture, execution model, tradeoff, or demonstrated learning
gap. The recommendation includes the topic, why it matters now, and the evidence
or decision context to teach. Children do not enter education mode or teach Lei
directly.

The coordinator decides whether teaching is useful and may suggest education
mode to Lei. Mechanical repairs do not warrant a suggestion. Enter the mode
only after Lei explicitly requests or accepts it. During the lesson, the
coordinator may send bounded supporting assignments to children, but retains
the interactive teaching conversation and remains the sole learner-state
writer.
